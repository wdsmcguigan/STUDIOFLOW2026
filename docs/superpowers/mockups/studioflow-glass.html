Review
The tradeoff to decide: glass looks gorgeous in a hero shot, but a film breakdown is a dense data surface — hundreds of rows where blur-behind-blur hurts contrast, legibility and (on big grids) scroll performance. My recommendation is a "glass chrome, solid content" middle path: frosted top bar, command palette, AI dock, side panels & modals (the floating layers you see here), but opaque stripboards, tables and forms underneath. Tell me which way you lean: (a) full glass everywhere, (b) glass chrome + solid content [recommended], or (c) back to mostly-opaque with subtle glass accents — and which of these four backdrops you like best.
